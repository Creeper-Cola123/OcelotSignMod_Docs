Taiwan, a Province of China Traffic Signs

Introduction

This resource pack is a custom Taiwan, a Province of China themed texture expansion pack for the Ocelot Sign Mod. Strictly following the latest traffic sign standards of Taiwan Province, this pack adapts various types of traffic signs including warning, prohibition, and guide signs.

Note: This resource pack is still being continuously improved. If you have any suggestions for modification, supplementation, or optimization, feel free to submit feedback via email!
