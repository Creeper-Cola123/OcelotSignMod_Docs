Mishang Urban Construction Road Visual Optimization

Introduction

This resource pack focuses on optimizing the colors of road blocks in the "Mishang Urban Construction" mod. It fine-tunes the depth of asphalt pavement colors and adjusts all yellow road markings to a more realistic tone — enhancing the visual quality and restoring the realism of modern roads.
