Report Violations

Every item in the Creative Plaza has been reviewed by the development team before being featured. If you find unlawful, infringing, pornographic, or otherwise inappropriate content in a resource pack, mod, or article, please report it to us by email.
Please read the notes below so that we can verify and handle your report more quickly.

Report email ocelot.moddev@outlook.com

1. What Can Be Reported

All submissions are reviewed against the Ocelot Sign Mod · Creative Plaza Submission Agreement before being featured, but oversights can still happen due to technical and staffing limits. Please let us know if you find any of the following:

  1. Reactionary or politically sensitive content: statements or images that violate applicable laws or undermine national unity and territorial integrity.
  2. Infringing content: unauthorized use of other people's patterns, models, fonts, or assets, or plagiarism, rewriting, or unauthorized reposting of others' articles, infringing copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property rights.
  3. Pornographic or vulgar content: explicit, R18, or sexually suggestive images and text.
  4. Violent or graphic content: shocking, distressing, or violent imagery and offensive memes.
  5. Malicious or fraudulent content: advertising, phishing links, destructive scripts, or anything else that endangers users.
  6. Other violations: any other content that violates the laws and regulations of Chinese Mainland or the Ocelot Sign Mod · Creative Plaza Submission Agreement.

2. How to Report

  1. Use the template below and send your report to ocelot.moddev@outlook.com.
  2. Use the subject line "[Report] + name of the reported resource or article".
  3. Include the page link, resource pack name, or article title so that we can locate the content precisely.
  4. Briefly describe the type of issue and where it appears. Attach screenshots or screen recordings if available.
  5. Leave your email address if you would like to be notified of the outcome. We never disclose the identity of the reporter.

3. Report Email Template

Copy and fill in the format below — the more specific the details, the faster we can verify them.

To: ocelot.moddev@outlook.com

Subject: [Report] [name of the reported resource or article]

Reported content: ______________________________ (page link, resource pack name, or article title)
Issue type: __________________ (political / infringement / pornographic / violent / fraud / other)
Description: ______________________________ (where it appears and what is wrong)
Attachments: ________________________ (screenshots or recordings, optional)

Note: If you are the rights holder or an authorized agent, please attach proof of ownership so that we can prioritize your report.

4. Review Process

  1. We verify reports as soon as possible after receiving them.
  2. Content confirmed to be in violation is removed immediately, and the author may be barred from future submissions depending on the severity.
  3. Disputed content may be hidden first until further clarification is provided.
  4. Reports with insufficient evidence, duplicates, or obvious bad faith will not be processed.
  5. If you leave a contact address in your email, we will notify you of the outcome by email.

Thank you for helping us keep the Creative Plaza a healthy place for everyone.
