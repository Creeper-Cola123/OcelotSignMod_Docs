A curated space showcasing high-quality community resource packs — discover interesting patterns, fonts, and custom model inspiration.

✉ Submit a resource pack via email

This is a curated space that showcases high-quality community resource packs. We will keep tracking community activity and select creative patterns to be featured in the Plaza. You are also welcome to develop traffic signs for other countries or regions using this mod.

To ensure that resource packs comply with Chinese Mainland laws and regulations, the Creative Plaza currently operates in a "curated submission" mode rather than open self-service upload. For high-quality resources reviewed and approved by the mod development team, we will promote them in the form of "original links from the authors". We apologize for the inconvenience and look forward to seeing more outstanding works!
