📧 Sign Resource Pack Submission Guide

Welcome! We invite all city-building enthusiasts and mod players to submit your crafted sign resource packs to the Ocelot Creeper Cola Development Group.
Please read the guidelines below carefully before submitting:

📬
Submission Email ocelot.moddev@outlook.com

1. Resource Pack Creation & Quality Requirements

1.1 Naming Conventions

  1. All contents must be placed under the ocelotsignmod namespace — file paths must start with assets/ocelotsignmod/.
  2. All folder and file names may only use lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores (e.g., my_custom_sign.png). Spaces, Chinese characters, or uppercase letters are prohibited.

1.2 Image & Model Specifications

  1. Texture files must be .png format with strict 1:1 aspect ratio. Recommended resolution: 512×512 or 256×256.
  2. Custom 3D models must follow the vanilla 16×16×16 block proportions. Naming convention: "[Resource Pack Name] Model Name", e.g., [Hong Kong, China] Linear Delineator A.

1.3 Advanced Custom UI Categorization

  1. Use "Advanced Custom UI" to create your own categories — do not dump all textures into the mod's default categories.
  2. Create a UI config file under ui_definitions (e.g., my_road_signs.json). Use "useSubfolders" and "filterMode" for detailed organization.
  3. All category names and code must use English; the actual textures may include Chinese or other languages.

1.4 Copyright & Open Source

  1. Please observe font and texture copyright. Seek free open-source alternatives (e.g., Source Han Sans, LXGW WenKai) if there is infringement risk.
  2. Choose a license: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, MIT, GPLv3, or "All Rights Reserved".

1.5 Sensitive Content Guidelines

  1. Ensure correct use of Chinese sovereignty terminology. Standard examples:
Chinese Mainland — 中国大陆
Hong Kong, China — 中国香港
Taiwan, a province of China — 中国台湾
  1. Do not include any national or regional flags/banners in your textures or models.

🚫 2. Prohibited Content (Immediate & Permanent Rejection)

The following is strictly prohibited in your resource pack and any accompanying files:

  • Reactionary or sensitive topics: Recent or historical sensitive social events of any country.
  • Political content: Politicians, political party symbols, slogans, or any political content.
  • National division: Any content implying or supporting division or undermining of territorial integrity.
  • Pornography: Any explicit, R18, or sexually suggestive images or text.
  • Vulgarity & violence: Bloody, disturbing, or violent imagery or vulgar memes.
  • Copyright infringement: Unauthorized use of resources from other games, mods, or paid asset libraries.
  • Malicious code or extraneous files: Advertisements, referral links, or malicious/destructive scripts.

📬 3. Submission Email Template

Send your email to ocelot.moddev@outlook.com using the template below:

To: ocelot.moddev@outlook.com

Subject: [Submission] [Your Resource Pack Name] — e.g.: [Submission] Hong Kong Traffic Resource Pack

Author: _________________________________ (Nickname/ID; optional Bilibili / GitHub / personal links)
Description: __________________________________ (Pack contents, number of signs/models, applicable scenarios)
License: ___________________________________________ (e.g., CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)

Attachment: Attach your .zip resource pack; if too large, include a permanent cloud storage link.

⏳ 4. Review & Curation Policy

  1. Not all submissions are guaranteed to be officially curated. Reviews are typically conducted once every 1–2 months.
  2. If your pack passes review and is selected, we will send a formal notification by email. If not selected, no reply will be sent — thank you for your understanding.
  3. For high-quality submissions with minor formatting issues (JSON errors, incorrect paths), the development team will assist with corrections.
  4. Serious copyright infringement, severely non-standard naming, or violation of the "Prohibited Content" list will result in outright rejection.

Thank you for your support and contributions to the Ocelot Sign Mod!
