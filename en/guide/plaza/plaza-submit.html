1. Resource Pack Creation & Quality Requirements

Naming Conventions

  1. All folder and file names may only use lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores (e.g., my_custom_sign.png). Spaces, Chinese characters, or uppercase letters are prohibited.

Image & Model Specifications

  1. Texture files must be .png format with strict 1:1 aspect ratio. Recommended resolution: 512×512 or 256×256.
  2. Naming convention: "[Resource Pack Name] Model Name", e.g., [Hong Kong, China] Linear Delineator A.

Advanced Custom UI Categorization

  1. Use "Advanced Custom UI" to create your own categories — do not dump all textures into the mod's default categories.
  2. Create a UI config file under ui_definitions (e.g., my_road_signs.json). Use "useSubfolders" and "filterMode" for detailed organization.
  3. All category names and code must use English; the actual textures may include Chinese or other languages.

Copyright & Open Source

  1. Please observe font and texture copyright. Seek free open-source alternatives (e.g., Source Han Sans, LXGW WenKai) if there is infringement risk.
  2. Choose a license: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, MIT, GPLv3, or "All Rights Reserved".

Sensitive Content Guidelines

  1. Ensure correct use of Chinese sovereignty terminology.
  2. Do not include any national or regional flags/banners in your textures or models.

2. Submission Email Template

Once your .zip resource pack is ready, send your email to ocelot.moddev@outlook.com using the template below:

To: ocelot.moddev@outlook.com

Subject: [Submission] [Your Resource Pack Name] — e.g.: [Submission] Hong Kong Traffic Resource Pack

Author: _________________________________ (Nickname/ID; optional Bilibili / GitHub / personal links)
Description: __________________________________ (Pack contents, number of signs/models, applicable scenarios)
License: ___________________________________________ (e.g., CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)

Attachment: Attach your .zip resource pack; if too large, include a permanent cloud storage link. You may also upload the pack to Modrinth or similar hosting platforms and submit the link afterwards.

1. Article Submissions

Besides resource packs, the Creative Plaza also features original articles related to the mod and city building. Tutorials, gameplay notes, resource pack creation experience, mod introductions, and crossover gameplay write-ups are all welcome.

  1. Articles must be your own original work or submitted with permission from the original author. Cite the source when quoting others' content.
  2. Accepted articles will be translated by the development team into both Chinese and English and published on the Chinese and English pages of this site. The author's attribution is retained.
  3. You can write the article directly in the email, or attach it as .md or .docx. Bundle images into a .zip or insert them in order in the body.
  4. Use the subject line "[Article Submission] + article title".

3. Prohibited Content (Immediate & Permanent Rejection)

The following is strictly prohibited in your resource pack, article, and any accompanying files:

  1. Recent or historical sensitive social events of any country.
  2. Politicians, political party symbols, slogans, or any political content.
  3. Any content implying or supporting division or undermining of territorial integrity.
  4. Any explicit, R18, or sexually suggestive images or text.
  5. Bloody, disturbing, or violent imagery or vulgar memes.
  6. Unauthorized use of resources from other games, mods, or paid asset libraries.
  7. Advertisements, referral links, or malicious/destructive scripts.

4. Review & Curation Policy

  1. Not all submissions are guaranteed to be officially curated. Reviews are typically conducted once every 1–2 months.
  2. If your pack passes review and is selected, we will send a formal notification by email. If not selected, no reply will be sent — thank you for your understanding.
  3. Serious copyright infringement, plagiarism, severely non-standard naming, or violation of the "Prohibited Content" list will result in outright rejection of the resource pack or article.

Thank you for your support and contributions to the Ocelot Sign Mod — we look forward to your resource packs and articles!
