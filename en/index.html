MishangUC

Mishang Urban Construction (MishangUC) is a Fabric-platform building and decoration mod that delivers an immersive, high-freedom city-building experience. The mod adds realistic road blocks with acceleration and auto-line marking, richly customizable lighting, flexible sign blocks, and a variety of railings. It also bundles a powerful toolset — quick build, force-place, block rotation/mirror, NBT and state viewer — that dramatically speeds up construction and debugging.

Ocelot Sign Mod

As a major expansion of the MishangUC ecosystem, Ocelot Sign Mod is a Fabric-platform sign mod focused on road traffic signage. It inherits MishangUC's flexible text and pattern editor and adds authentic road sign art based on the Chinese Mainland standard, plus a rich set of public-info symbols, dedicated editable road sign blocks, and matching sign posts. On top of the road blocks, it adds special "advance guide arrows" on the ground, eye-catching orange arrows, and various prohibition arrows to flesh out the on-road traffic guidance. Combined with the official Pattern & Font list, players can browse and call up the entire library of resources.

This mod is an add-on of MishangUC. Per MishangUC's open-source license, this mod is open-sourced under LGPL-3.0. Third-party fonts and pre-existing code modifications inside the mod follow their original OFL-1.1 and LGPL-3.0 licenses — see the LICENSE file in the repository for details.
