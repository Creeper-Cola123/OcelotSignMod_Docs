# Mod Introduction

Mishang Urban Construction (MishangUC) is a building and decoration mod designed exclusively for the Fabric platform, aiming to provide players with a highly immersive and freely creative urban construction experience. In addition to immersive road blocks with speed boosts and automatic line marking functions, colorful customizable lights, highly adjustable signs, and various types of railings, the mod also features a powerful built-in utility system. With practical tools such as quick building, forced placement, block rotation and mirroring, and NBT data & status inspection, players can greatly improve their building and debugging efficiency.

As an important expansion within the Mishang Urban Construction ecosystem, the Ocelot Sign Mod is a professional signage add-on also developed for the Fabric platform. Centered on road traffic guidance, it fully inherits the highly flexible text and graphic editing system from Mishang Urban Construction, bringing more realistic traffic details to players' city builds. The mod comes with deeply recreated road sign graphics based on those used in the Chinese mainland, along with a large collection of practical public information symbols. It adds dedicated, fully customizable road sign blocks and matching sign posts. Building on existing road blocks, it also supplements on-road advance directional arrows, bright orange arrows, and various prohibition arrows, further refining on-road traffic guidance details. Using the official Graphic and Font List (see Yuque reference documentation), players can easily browse and freely use a massive library of graphics.
